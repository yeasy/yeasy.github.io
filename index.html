Baohua Yang

Agentic AI · Distributed Systems · Security

Resume LinkedIn GitHub Google Scholar

Baohua Yang is a systems architect and technical leader who has spent nearly two decades turning frontier research into products at scale.

  • Agentic AI & Security: Architected and shipped an AI agent for Oracle TSAM that diagnoses failures from monitoring data, raising accuracy from ~30% to 90%+, and built its safety layer against prompt injection and data leakage.
  • Distributed Systems: Helped build one of the first enterprise blockchain platforms (Hyperledger Fabric) at IBM, then architected Oracle Blockchain Platform, scaling it to 10,000 TPS and several hundred tenants.
  • High Performance: Designed the packet-processing algorithm behind a 10 Gbps UTM gateway in 2007.
  • Open Source: Founding contributor to Hyperledger and three-term member of its Technical Steering Committee; maintainer of several Hyperledger projects.
  • Publications: 20+ papers (IEEE INFOCOM, ACM HotNets, IEEE Transactions on Computers), 10 granted patents, and open-source books with 100M+ reads.
