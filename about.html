About

I'm a systems architect who enjoys the space between a research idea and a system people rely on every day. I started out in high-performance networking, moved into enterprise blockchain at IBM Research, and today I work on agentic AI at Oracle, with a focus on making AI agents reliable and safe enough for production.

Writing is how I learn. Most of my open-source books began as notes to myself, and I still write guides whenever a new field catches my attention, from Docker and blockchain to agentic AI.
