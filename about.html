Baohua Yang

Agentic AI · Distributed Systems · Security

Baohua Yang is a systems architect and technical leader with nearly two decades of turning research into production systems, from 10 Gbps network security and enterprise blockchain to agentic AI.

He is a Principal Architect at Oracle, where he owns the architecture of Oracle Blockchain Platform and Oracle TSAM and builds enterprise AI agents and their safety layer. Before Oracle, he was a Research Scientist at IBM Research and an early designer of Hyperledger Fabric. He is a founding contributor to Hyperledger and served three terms on its Technical Steering Committee.

He has published 20+ papers (IEEE INFOCOM, ACM HotNets, IEEE Transactions on Computers) and holds 10 granted patents. His open-source books, including Docker Practice (26K+ GitHub stars) and Blockchain Guide (7K+), have 100M+ reads, and he has 4,800+ followers on GitHub.

He holds a Ph.D. from Tsinghua University and was a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley.
